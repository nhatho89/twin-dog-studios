Saigon Street Brawl

In Development

Brew Chaos. Clear the Streets. Claim the Corporate Underworld.

Caffeine-Fueled Arcade Beat-'Em-Up Action

Set deep in the neon-lit alleys, bustling street corners, and high-rise districts of Saigon, Saigon Street Brawl: Caffeine Rush combines high-octane arcade brawler action with hyper-charged coffee corporate warfare.

Master fast-paced combo chains, defend your local brew stands from rival syndicates, and climb your way from pavement coffee carts to the top executive seat of the caffeine empire.

Key Highlights & Features

Currently in active development at Twin Dog Studios, Saigon Street Brawl is being crafted to bring classic side-scrolling beat-'em-up nostalgia into a vibrant, modern setting:

  • Fast-Paced Combo Combat: String together rapid punches, heavy strikes, special coffee-boosted power moves, and environmental counters to clear out swarms of rival enforcers.
  • Saigon-Inspired Arenas: Battle through stylized urban environments inspired by Saigon's iconic street food markets, hidden alleyways, and glowing rooftop lounges.
  • Territory & Stall Defense: Protect your mobile brew stands and upgrade your street spots as you expand your faction's influence district by district.
  • Expressive Character Roster: Choose from a roster of unique brawlers, each bringing distinct fighting styles, special finishers, and personal storylines.

Development Roadmap

We are actively building core gameplay loops, character animations, and level designs. Stay tuned for upcoming developer logs, playtest signups, and early teaser trailers as we move closer to our first playable demo!
