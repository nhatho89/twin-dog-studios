Any Game. Any Rule. Any Place. Play Without Limits.

Freestyle Cards redefines digital tabletop gaming by giving you total freedom over the cards. Whether you are inventing a brand new card game or just looking for a versatile sandbox environment to play classic card games with your friends online, Freestyle Cards adapts entirely to your rules.

Designed with intuitive multiplayer tools and a fluid user interface, the application empowers creators and players alike to break the boundaries of physical card restrictions. Build your layouts, set your parameters and invite your group for game night from anywhere in the world. Available now on the Apple App Store.
