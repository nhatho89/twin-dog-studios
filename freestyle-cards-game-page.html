Freestyle Cards

November 2026  |  iOS App Store

Any Game. Any Rule. Any Place. Play Without Limits.

A True Digital Tabletop Sandbox

Freestyle Cards redefines digital tabletop gaming by giving you total freedom over the felt. Whether you are inventing a brand new card game from scratch, testing homebrew mechanics, or looking for an open sandbox environment to play classic card games with your friends online, Freestyle Cards adapts entirely to your vision.

Instead of locking you into rigid automated game loops, enforced turn structures, or scripted winning conditions, Freestyle Cards acts as a real world physical deck on a virtual table. You control the cards, you set the parameters, and your group enforces the house rules—just like gathered around a kitchen table on game night.

Core Features & Table Control

Designed with fluid, tactile interactions and an intuitive multiplayer toolkit, the app empowers players to break free from traditional digital card game restrictions:

  • Total Tabletop Freedom: Drag, flip, stack, rotate, and deal cards freely across the central play area. Move individual cards or select groups with responsive multi card controls.
  • Granular Dealing & Hand Controls: Deal specified card counts directly to individual player seats or distribute evenly across all participants with a single tap. Instantly sort, shuffle, or clear your hand whenever needed.
  • Custom Room Rules & Conditions: Configure card visibility on the fly. Toggle whether drawn cards default to face down, hide hands for hidden information games, or reveal all hands for open information practice sessions. Save your room presets to instantly reload custom game setups in future matches.
  • Real Time Multiplayer & Session Persistence: Host private or open tables using unique Game IDs. Real time state synchronization keeps room state, player hands, and active card stacks perfectly aligned across all connected devices.
  • Table Customization Engine: Tailor your play space with custom felt colors, border palettes, background atmospheres, and custom card deck designs. Save your favorite visual themes to create distinct gaming atmospheres.
  • Expressive Table Communication: Stay engaged during heated matches with live chat and instant reaction banners that display active feedback directly over player seats and game logs.

Premium Upgrades & Host Perks

While the core card sandbox is completely free to play, Freestyle Cards offers optional premium upgrades—available as a monthly subscription or a one time Lifetime Purchase—as well as standalone deck collections to elevate your gaming experience:

  • Ad Free Tabletop for Everyone: When the host of a room holds an active Premium Subscription or Lifetime Pass, advertisements are completely removed for all connected players at that table for the entire session.
  • Custom Center Table Branding: Premium members get their studio or player name permanently emblazoned across the center felt of their virtual table.
  • Custom Table Sharing: Design your ultimate tabletop layout with personalized color palettes, felt textures, and ambient backgrounds, then share your custom table design live with everyone in your room.
  • Standalone Deck Purchases: In addition to premium features, players can purchase individual thematic card decks from minimalist suits to vibrant geometric artwork to customize their personal hand and table aesthetics without needing a subscription.
  • Seamless Cross Device Restores: All premium passes and deck purchases are tied securely to your Apple / Google account, allowing you to restore your unlocks on any device with a single tap.

Regional Availability

Freestyle Cards is available for download on iOS worldwide across supported App Store regions, excluding Vietnam and Mainland China.
