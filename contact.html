Behind Twin Dog Studios

Twin Dog Studios is a solo indie studio run by me, Nhat. I handle everything from game design and code to managing caffeine levels.

Named in memory of my two beloved dogs, the studio's mission is simple: craft fun, memorable games with soul.

Whether it's building sandbox card games like Freestyle Cards or beat-'em-ups like Saigon Street Brawl: Caffeine Rush, we're dedicated to bringing unique indie experiences to life.

Get in Touch

Whether you're experiencing a bug on iOS, have feedback on our card mechanics, or want to collaborate on indie game development, our inbox is always open. Reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
